# Super-resolution Review And Decision Tree

This review records branch-local logic for the 11-method candidate freeze. It does not define a cross-branch universal ordering, universal method choice, execution readiness, machine-call contract, backend binding, or execution-environment bundle.

## Logic Review

The 2026-05-04 taxonomy cleanup uses Layer 1 `Main Input`, `Main Output`, and `Analysis Problem Basis` fields to keep Super-resolution, Gene Expression Prediction / Imputation, and Denoising / Signal Recovery separated by input/output object rather than by promotional wording.

## Decision Tree

1. **Measured-anchor gate.** If the task lacks target-sample measured spatial expression or spatial omics anchoring the output, route to Gene Expression Prediction / Imputation rather than Super-resolution.
2. **Geometry gate.** Consider Super-resolution when the output asks for subspot, high-density, pseudo-cell, cell-level, or submicron/factor geometry of the same sample signal. For FICTURE, interpret the branch as measured high-density/submicron factor representation, not generated upsampling.
3. **Histology branch.** Consider image-aware rows such as iStar, TESLA, XFuse, ImSpiRE, scstGCN, Spotiphy, iSCALE, or Thor when matched histology is central to the requested reconstruction.
4. **Generated-output caveat.** For STAGE, Spotiphy, scstGCN, iSCALE, Thor, and similar rows, treat high-density, pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, or cell-level outputs as inferred reconstructions, not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.

## Coverage Ledger

- `FICTURE`: submicron or high-density measured spatial transcriptomics data -> segmentation-free spatial factor and tissue-feature maps. Caveat: Factor maps can be mistaken for cell-resolved expression if the downstream reader ignores the segmentation-free modeling frame.
- `iStar`: target-sample spot-based spatial transcriptomics with matched histology -> super-resolved tissue architecture and inferred fine-resolution expression map. Caveat: Histology-guided inference can overfit morphology and may under-represent molecular programs that are weakly visible in the image. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `scstGCN`: target-sample spot-based ST, spatial coordinates, and histology image -> inferred single-cell-resolution spatial gene-expression map. Caveat: Graph and image fusion can propagate image-driven or neighborhood-driven assumptions into expression estimates; validation should not rely only on visual plausibility. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `Spotiphy`: target-sample section-level spatial transcriptomics with image/context features -> pseudo-single-cell whole-transcriptome spatial map. Caveat: Pseudo-single-cell reconstruction can create a false sense of measured cellular certainty; downstream claims should separate inferred cells from assayed molecules. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `TESLA`: target-sample spatial transcriptomics with histology image -> super-resolved spatial expression and tissue-ecosystem maps. Caveat: Tumor-focused validation may not transfer cleanly to non-tumor tissues, and morphology-linked signals can reinforce tumor-region priors. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `XFuse`: target-sample spatial transcriptomics with matched histology -> super-resolved spatial expression map. Caveat: Deep fusion can hallucinate image-correlated expression patterns; method-paper validation does not by itself prove biological correctness on new tissues. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `ImSpiRE`: target-sample spatial transcriptomics with histology image features -> image-aided enhanced-resolution spatial expression map. Caveat: Image-guided redistribution and unmeasured-region imputation can create plausible subspot maps without direct molecular measurement; validation should separate image agreement from expression truth. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `soScope`: target-sample spatial omics data with cross-platform enhancement context -> enhanced-resolution tissue characterization or expression representation. Caveat: Generative enhanced-resolution profiles can encode distributional priors, image correlations, or smoothing artifacts; simulation recovery does not by itself prove biological correctness on new tissues. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `iSCALE`: large-area target-sample spatial transcriptomics with tissue architecture context -> cellular-level inferred tissue architecture and reconstructed expression map. Caveat: Large-area reconstruction can hide local failure modes when evaluated only by broad tissue-level agreement. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `STAGE`: sparse target-sample spatial transcriptomics inputs -> generated high-density spatial transcriptomics profiles. Caveat: Generated high-density profiles require checks against smoothing, spatial autocorrelation artifacts, and circular validation against generated targets. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
- `Thor`: target-sample spatial transcriptomics with matched histology -> histology-aware cell-level spatial transcriptomics representation. Caveat: Thor is a platform/workflow row; cell-level outputs are inferred and platform-context dependent, not direct molecule-level or segmentation-validated ground truth. Outputs are inferred reconstructions anchored to target-sample measured spatial data; pseudo-cell, single-cell-resolution, high-density, or cell-level outputs are not direct molecule-level single-cell measurements.
