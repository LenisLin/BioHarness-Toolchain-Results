## Comparator Clusters

`Cluster A: Graph-neighborhood encoders`

- Tools: `ADEPT`, `CCST`, `GraphST`, `SEDR`, `SpaceFlow`, `STAGATE`
- Rationale: graph-based neighborhood encoders for domain calling, mainly without required morphology.

`Cluster B: Image-informed graph and multimodal domain callers`

- Tools: `ConGI`, `DeepST`, `SiGra`, `SpaGCN`, `stLearn`
- Rationale: morphology or image-augmented integration is central or decision-relevant.

`Cluster C: Bayesian, probabilistic, and latent-model domain callers`

- Tools: `BASS`, `BayesSpace`, `DR-SC`, `PRECAST`, `SpatialPCA`
- Rationale: domains emerge from Bayesian smoothing, probabilistic alignment, or spatially structured embedding.

`Cluster D: Multiscale and tissue-architecture structure methods`

- Tools: `BANKSY`, `MENDER`, `SPACEL`, `SpaSEG`
- Rationale: emphasize multiscale context, tissue architecture, slice structure, or scalable structural segmentation-style domain discovery.

`Cluster E: Topic, semantic, and interpretable domain discovery`

- Tools: `Pianno`, `PROST`, `SpaTopic`, `STAMP`
- Rationale: domains are mediated by semantic priors, interpretable topics, gene programs, or pattern modules.

`Cluster F: Hybrid edge-case methods`

- Tools: `STCC`, `SpatialPrompt`, `conST`
- Rationale: nearest comparators bridge multiple clusters rather than fitting one clean family.

Cluster note:

- `STCC` is retained in this topic as a post-processing / consensus method rather than a typical standalone first-pass domain model, `SpatialPrompt` stays as a fast first-pass edge-case method, and `conST` is treated as a bridge row between graph-contrastive and image-aware multimodal choices.
- Their `Closest Alternatives` and related selection cues remain pilot provisional Layer 2 judgment and therefore stay intentionally conservative.

## Consistency Review

- `Closest Alternatives` stay cluster-consistent for the main five clusters, while `STCC`, `SpatialPrompt`, and `conST` keep intentionally conservative edge-case comparators because they sit at the hybrid boundary.
- `Key Difference` statements were kept at the decision-axis level and should still be read as pilot provisional local-comparison judgment.
- `Best First Choice When` and `Do Not Start With This When` were checked to make sure they describe different selection conditions rather than simple negations.
- `Memory Burden` was kept at `unclear` across the full topic because direct public support stayed weak even after second-pass retrieval.
- `Scale Sensitivity` was only made more specific when papers or official materials explicitly emphasized scalability.
- `Main Spatial Prior` stayed as a single Layer 2 decision field using `primary label + optional short modifier`, with the primary label drawn from the frozen label set.
- `Cross-slice Support` and `Batch Handling` were filled conservatively and remain pilot provisional, especially for tools whose papers mention multi-sample use without a fully explicit joint model.
