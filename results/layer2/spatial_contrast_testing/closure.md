## Remaining Caveats

- `STcompare` is retained for structurally matched spatial-pattern contrast; its preprint status is evidence provenance and confidence caveat, not a Layer 2 selection gate.
- Direct contrast tests are not interchangeable with phenotype-linked scores, motifs, clinical niches, tissue cellular neighborhood features, or within-sample SVG detection.
- Registration, structural matching, niche definition, deconvolution, spatial autocorrelation, and replicate/covariate design can all create false contrast signal.
