# Layer 1 Task Catalog

This catalog is the compact Layer 1 task-name interface for BioHarness spatial transcriptomics work. It is for problem splitting and agent context reduction only. It does not list methods, rank methods, route to Layer 2 artifacts, or make execution claims.

## Preprocessing

Problem boundary: Prepare spatial transcriptomics data for downstream analysis by addressing data quality, artifact, and preprocessing needs before scientific task selection.
Typical output: A cleaned, checked, or preprocessing-ready spatial data object or report.

## Segmentation

Problem boundary: Define cells, spots, transcripts, or spatial units from imaging-based or spatial molecular measurements.
Typical output: Segmentation labels, cell or unit boundaries, or transcript-to-cell assignments.

## Super-resolution

Problem boundary: Increase spatial resolution or infer finer-grained spatial structure from lower-resolution spatial transcriptomics data.
Typical output: A higher-resolution expression representation or refined spatial tissue map.

## Spatial Gene Prediction

Problem boundary: Predict or complete gene expression across spatial locations, modalities, or missing measurement regions.
Typical output: Predicted gene expression values or completed spatial expression maps.

## Integration

Problem boundary: Align, combine, or jointly model multiple spatial samples, slices, conditions, technologies, or coordinate systems.
Typical output: Integrated embeddings, aligned coordinates, or harmonized multi-sample spatial representations.

## Graph / Neighborhood

Problem boundary: Represent local spatial neighborhoods, tissue niches, or spatial graphs for downstream neighborhood-level analysis.
Typical output: Neighborhood features, spatial graphs, niche labels, or local-context representations.

## Domain / Clustering

Problem boundary: Identify spatially coherent tissue domains, regions, clusters, or structural compartments.
Typical output: Domain labels or spatial cluster assignments.

## Spatially Variable Gene Detection

Problem boundary: Identify genes whose expression patterns vary systematically across spatial coordinates, regions, or cell-type-specific spatial contexts.
Typical output: Ranked spatially variable genes or spatial pattern gene sets.

## Cell Type Inference

Problem boundary: Infer cell type identities, proportions, placements, or annotations in spatial transcriptomics data.
Typical output: Cell type labels, cell type proportions, or spatial cell-type maps.

## Cell-Cell Communication

Problem boundary: Analyze spatially constrained interactions, communication signals, or neighborhood effects between cell types or tissue compartments.
Typical output: Interaction scores, communication networks, or spatially variable interaction signals.

## Comparative Analysis

Problem boundary: Compare spatial molecular patterns across conditions, groups, niches, domains, or tissue contexts.
Typical output: Differential spatial features, condition-associated effects, or comparison statistics.

## Spatial Trajectory Analysis

Problem boundary: Infer spatially organized transitions, trajectories, dynamics, or lineage-like progressions across tissue space.
Typical output: Spatial trajectories, transition maps, or ordered spatial states.

## Spatial Clonal Analysis

Problem boundary: Analyze spatial organization of clones, subclones, copy-number states, or lineage-derived tumor structures.
Typical output: Spatial clone labels, subclone maps, or clonal composition summaries.

## Spatial Perturbation Analysis

Problem boundary: Model spatial responses to perturbations, interventions, genetic screens, or experimentally altered tissue states.
Typical output: Perturbation-response effects, spatial response maps, or niche-aware perturbation summaries.

## Program Discovery

Problem boundary: Discover multicellular programs, latent tissue states, coordinated expression programs, or spatially organized functional modules.
Typical output: Program scores, latent factors, module assignments, or spatial program maps.
